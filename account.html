Download a copy

Save everything you have put into ElevateEdu as one file — assignments, grades, notes, workouts, journals, all of it. Handy as a backup, or if you ever want to move your work somewhere else.

Sign out

Signs you out on this device and clears the copy kept here. Your work stays saved to your account, so it comes back when you sign in again.

Delete everything

This clears your saved work from this device and from the cloud, and asks for your sign-in account to be removed. It cannot be undone, so download a copy first if there is any chance you will want it.
