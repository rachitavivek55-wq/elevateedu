What everything does

ElevateEdu is one thing: a planner for school. You do not have to use every tool in it — open the one you need and ignore the rest. Everything you put in is yours, saved to your own account.

Planner

The school side of things.

CalendarAdd commitments, assignments, exams and tasks, and see your whole month at a glance.
ChecklistsBreak big jobs into small checklists and tick them off as you go. Good for anything you repeat.
AssignmentsEvery assignment with its due date, so nothing gets forgotten.
GradebookRecord your scores and watch your average for each class. One book per school year, then terms, then classes with weighted categories inside. Marks can be letters, a percentage, points out of a total, or a report-card scale — 1 to 4 standards, 1 to 3 proficiency, IB 1 to 7, GPA points, or pass/fail.
NotesKeep your class notes tidy in sections you choose, with photos attached where they help.
How saving works. Everything is tied to the account you signed in with, so it follows you from your phone to a laptop and back. It also keeps working with no signal — anything you change offline is sent up the next time you are connected.
