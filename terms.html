What ElevateEdu is

ElevateEdu is a free study organiser for students. It gives you a planner, a calendar, a vision board and a set of wellness and mindset tools in one place. It is built and run by an individual student, not by a company.

It is free, and it stays free

Every feature is free. There is no paid tier, no trial that runs out and nothing to upgrade. We do not ask for card details and we never charge you for anything.

Your account

You sign in with your Google account, or with a one-time link sent to your email. There is no password for us to store, and we never see your Google password. Please keep your own email and Google account secure, because anyone who can open those can open your ElevateEdu account.

Your content belongs to you

Anything you put into the app, such as assignments, notes and images on your vision board, stays yours. We store it so that it syncs to your other devices. We do not sell it, publish it, or use it to train anything.

Using the app fairly

Please use ElevateEdu lawfully. Do not upload content that is illegal or that you do not have the right to upload, and do not try to break, overload or interfere with the service or with anyone else's account.

Leaving

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from inside the app, in Settings. Once it is deleted the data is gone and cannot be brought back.

No warranty

ElevateEdu is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and to keep your data safe, but we cannot promise the service will never be unavailable or that nothing will ever go wrong. Please do not rely on it as the only copy of anything important to you.
