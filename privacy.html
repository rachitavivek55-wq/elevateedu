Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

Privacy Policy

ElevateEdu ("we", "the app") helps students organize schoolwork. We collect only the information needed to run the app: your email address (for sign-in) and the data you create in the app, such as notes, tasks, and checklists.

Your data is stored securely using Supabase and is used only to provide the app to you. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details; Stripe handles them directly.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app: open the app, scroll to the bottom of the Home screen, and tap "Delete account". This permanently removes your data from our systems.

Terms of Service

ElevateEdu is provided as-is for personal, educational use. Premium features are available through an optional subscription of $5 per month, billed monthly, which you can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future charges; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Please use the app lawfully and do not attempt to disrupt or misuse the service.

Contact

For questions about your privacy or these terms, contact the app owner at the email associated with this account.
